# Traceable to Quanta

Everything AI generates traces back to the quanta it came from: the nodes in the Brain, and the passages inside their sources. No AI sentence stands without a path back to evidence. A design principle under [[See the Evidence]].

Each insight on an [[Insight Cards|Insight Card]] carries a confidence score and a link to the passage it rests on. The [[Document Viewer]] opens that passage, highlighted, with the AI Summary beside it. A [[Query]]'s Analysis is built from the insights gathered into it, and [[Export Query]] keeps sources beside every claim in the board material.

This rules out AI output that can only be taken on trust: a summary with no sources, a confidence score with nothing behind it, or a claim in an export that can't be walked back to a document.

The test is whether the director can always ask "where does that come from" and land on a source, not a description of one.
