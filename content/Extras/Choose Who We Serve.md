# Choose Who We Serve

Quantum State picks a place on the spectrum from regulated to unregulated, and accepts what that costs. It cannot serve everyone at once.

On the [[Tech]] side, the spectrum runs from an appliance on a bank's own premises to a shared cluster serving unregulated small businesses; each end asks for a different cost base and a different promise about where data lives. On the [[Business]] side, the same choice shows up in go-to-market: a paid pilot sold to a regulated board's governance sponsor looks nothing like a low-cost hosted service for small businesses. Building for both ends at once means running two cost bases and answering to two very different buyers.

This rules out a roadmap that promises the highest end's data controls at the lowest end's price, or a sales motion that tries to win a DIFC-regulated firm and an unregulated SME with the same pitch. Picking a place on the spectrum means naming, for now, who Quantum State does not serve.
