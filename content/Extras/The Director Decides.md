# The Director Decides

AI proposes; the director accepts or rejects, in the interface itself. Nothing AI suggests changes a Query, its Analysis or the board material until the director says yes. This is [[Human Authority]] applied screen by screen.

A suggested change in [[AI Chat]] waits for the director to accept or reject it. AI-suggested insights in a [[Query]] only join the graph when chosen. [[Export Query]] shows a draft preview before anything is exported, and the director sets its scope and level of detail.

This rules out AI that edits work silently, auto-applies its own suggestions, or sends material onward without a person reviewing it.

**Used by:** [[Design]], [[Product]]. Features: [[AI Chat]], [[Query]], [[Export Query]].
