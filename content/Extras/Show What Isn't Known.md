# Show What Isn't Known

What Quantum State doesn't know is shown, not smoothed over. Gaps, stale data and uncertainty are part of the picture the director sees, in the same place as the findings they qualify. A design principle under [[See the Evidence]].

Insights carry confidence scores. A [[Query]] shows its readiness, including whether financial data is historical or live. [[Export Query]] keeps evidence and uncertainty beside each claim in the board material. While sources are still being read, the [[Governance Brain]] shows that the build is unfinished rather than presenting a partial graph as complete.

This rules out a confident answer where the evidence is thin, a figure with no date or source, and a clean board summary that leaves out what couldn't be checked. When financial data is limited, the product says so instead of implying a full picture.
