# A Candid Assistant

AI acts as a candid governance assistant. It says what the evidence shows even when it is unwelcome, raises what the director hasn't asked about, and shows disagreement openly instead of blending it into one answer. Candor never becomes authority: the AI advises, and [[Human Authority]] keeps every decision with people.

In the [[Board Simulation Room]], several agents work through a scenario, and Artifact Review shows where they agree and where they disagree, with each agent's reasoning underneath. [[AI Chat]] answers questions in the context of the screen it opens from. Suggested insights and suggested focuses in a [[Query]] point to material the director may have missed.

This rules out an assistant that only confirms what the director already thinks, averages disagreement into false consensus, or softens a finding to keep it comfortable.
