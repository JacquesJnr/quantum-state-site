# Present Without Bias

Governance data is presented without bias. The interface shows what the evidence says, including what supports a reading, what contradicts it and what is missing, and never arranges it to lean the director toward a conclusion. A design principle under [[See the Evidence]].

The [[Governance Brain]] opens on the full, unfiltered graph. Filters and search narrow the view only when the director asks, and the Governance Overview Card suggests issues without hiding the rest. A node's weight shows through its connections, not through styling that marks it as important.

This rules out a default view that pre-selects "what matters", highlighting that ranks one reading above another without saying why, and summaries that drop contradicting evidence to read more cleanly.
