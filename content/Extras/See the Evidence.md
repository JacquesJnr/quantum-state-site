# See the Evidence

Quantum State is visual first: governance data is shown as its own relational graph. Evidence is presented without bias, so supporting, contradicting and missing evidence each get equal weight, and the interface never leans Morgan toward a conclusion. Every node, insight and claim traces back to its source.

The [[Governance Brain]] shows the whole graph, not a filtered version that favors one reading. A [[Node Details Panel|node's details]] and an [[Insight Cards|Insight Card]] carry a confidence level next to the claim, so a well-supported finding and a thin one don't look the same. Opening the [[Document Viewer]] takes Morgan to the exact source passage behind an insight, not a summary of it.

This rules out an AI answer with no path back to its source, a confidence score with nothing behind it, and a result that quietly drops the evidence that contradicts it. Missing evidence gets named as missing, not hidden by a confident-sounding sentence.

The practical test is whether Morgan can always ask "why does it say that" and land on a document, not a description of one.

**Design principles under it:** [[Present Without Bias]], [[Traceable to Quanta]], [[Show What Isn't Known]].
