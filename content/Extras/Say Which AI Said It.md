# Say Which AI Said It

Every AI-generated claim shows which model produced it, and any handoff to a model outside the customer's boundary is visible to the director before it happens. A design principle under [[Data Sovereignty]] and [[Traceable to Quanta]].

The app map does not yet show this. Scenario Setup in the [[Board Simulation Room]] lets the director choose the model for a run, and [[Tech]] sets out options where a local model does most of the work and a frontier model handles the hardest cases. When that happens, the destination, the material being sent and the purpose need to be visible, with a way to decline, and a mark stays on the answer and on the export.

This rules out answers with no record of the model behind them, and material leaving the building without the director knowing.
