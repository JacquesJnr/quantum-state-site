The material the [[MVP]] prototype needs, and the steps it goes through before any AI tool reads it.

## Period

- [ ] At least one year of material
- [ ] Ideally three years, so drift and dropped topics show across packs

## Board material

Grouped the way the board receives it.

- [ ] **Board:** packs, minutes and resolutions
- [ ] **Audit committee:** packs, minutes, external audit reports
- [ ] **Remuneration and nominations committee:** packs and minutes
- [ ] **Financials:** management accounts, audited statements, budgets
- [ ] **Internal audit:** plans, reports, findings and their follow-up
- [ ] **Compliance:** reports, breaches, regulatory correspondence

## Policies

The base for checking claims. Without them, an anomaly has nothing to be measured against.

- [ ] Compliance manual
- [ ] Remuneration policy
- [ ] Risk appetite statement and risk register
- [ ] Delegation of authority
- [ ] Any other policy the packs refer to

## Anonymisation

- [ ] Replace names of people, the company and counterparties with consistent placeholders: the same person is always "Director A" and the same unit is always "Business Unit 1"
- [ ] Keep every number, date and currency unchanged
- [ ] Keep a private key of placeholders, held by the data owner and never shared with AI tools
- [ ] Spot-check a sample of documents for names left in headers, footers, signatures and file metadata

## Consent

- [ ] The data owner agrees in writing to the use of the material for the prototype
- [ ] The consent states that anonymised material goes to cloud AI tools
- [ ] The board secretary knows the material is being shared
- [ ] Agree what happens to the material and the prototype once the review ends

## Handover

- [ ] Material delivered in its original formats (PDF, Word, Excel), unsorted if that is how it exists
- [ ] A note of anything known to be missing, such as quarters or committees with no record
