# Keep the Director Moving

The director is never left waiting on the machine. Long jobs run in the background, progress is visible, and there is always something useful to do next.

During Connect the dots, the director can continue into the [[Governance Brain]] while the remaining sources finish reading, and the Brain shows the build still in progress. Each source shows as queued, reading, done or needing attention.

This rules out blocking screens during long AI work, and progress hidden behind a spinner. It matters more if models run locally, where a reply can take seconds or sit in a queue: progress, scope and a way to cancel keep the director oriented.
