# Export Investigation

**Built on:** [[Human Authority]], [[See the Evidence]]

Export Investigation turns a reviewed [[Board Simulation Room]] artifact into board-ready material Morgan can take outside Quantum State. It comes last in the journey because the board should see only what has survived every earlier stage: sourced, checked, and tested against other views.

![[x/Images/App Maps/Mo-28-Sep QS App Map 2.0 S7 Prepare.png]]

## What it does for Morgan

Preparing for the board opens [Export Investigation](https://www.figma.com/board/WITVErwq2RH0fhCbBqVAJs?node-id=13-4331), where Morgan chooses a detail level — summary, standard or full evidence — and picks how to send the material, by email, integration or file. Drafting opens [Draft preview](https://www.figma.com/board/WITVErwq2RH0fhCbBqVAJs?node-id=13-4332): the board-ready material as it will export, with sources, confidence and risk context kept beside each claim rather than smoothed away. Morgan can change the scope and return to redraft, or export the material, and an export notice confirms the file's name and format once it's done.

The point of the two-step flow is that nothing gets simplified into false confidence on the way out. Evidence, uncertainty and the financial basis behind a claim travel with it into the export, not just the conclusion.

## Conditions to use it

- A reviewed Board Simulation Room artifact, or at minimum a generated Analysis, is expected before there's material worth exporting.
- Detail level and scope can be changed and redrafted before the final export, without losing the underlying evidence.
- The journey stops at export. Sharing the file with the rest of the board happens through tools Quantum State doesn't draw.

## Still open

- Which export formats and fidelity are necessary for a first version isn't decided.
- What an export that fails looks like, and how the preview surfaces missing context before Morgan sends it, isn't specified.
