# What is Quantum State

> [!success] Vision Statement
> A continuous, evidence-grounded governance intelligence system built on AI-native technology for faster board-level attention, challenge, and judgment.

> [!example] Mission Statement
> To transform executive decision making for corporate governance by turning fragmented corporate evidence into trusted, reviewable insights so boards and executives can see what matters, understand why it matters, and make better-informed decisions.

## Product in One Paragraph

Quantum State is a secure governance intelligence platform that helps boards, executives, and governance teams see what matters, why it matters, what evidence supports it, what uncertainty remains, and what human judgment is required. It brings in fragmented finance, audit, risk, compliance, strategy, performance, policy, and governance material and builds it into a Governance Vault. From there, a user finds the territory that matters, gathers it into an Investigation around one question, has AI analyze it, tests what could change in the Board Simulation Room, and exports board-ready material. It does not replace governance authority. AI may surface, classify, summarize, compare, infer, and propose. Humans validate, escalate, approve, accept risk, change rules, and make formal decisions.
