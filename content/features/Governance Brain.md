# Governance Brain

**Built on:** [[See the Evidence]], [[Present Without Bias]], [[Show What Isn't Known]], [[Keep the Director Moving]]

The Governance Brain shows the whole [[Governance Vault]] as a graph. It works the way the graph view on this wiki does: every node is a policy, decision, report or risk, every line is how those things relate, and a node with many lines is one that a lot of other material depends on. Morgan looks here before reading anything in detail, because a matter's importance often shows in its connections before it shows in any single document.

## What it does for Morgan

[Governance Brain](https://www.figma.com/board/WITVErwq2RH0fhCbBqVAJs?node-id=13-4294) opens on the default graph view: the full, unfiltered vault, with a Governance Overview Card summarizing what's new and what AI suggests deserves attention. A Search Bar finds material by name, tag or content, and a Node Lens Filter narrows the graph to a domain or timeframe. An AI Chat Bar sits on the same screen for a direct question, and a build-progress indicator keeps showing if the vault is still finishing in the background.

From here Morgan views a node, which opens the [[Node Details Panel]] and moves him into stage 4 of the journey — understanding the evidence a node holds rather than just its position in the graph.

## Conditions to use it

- The Governance Vault has to exist first; the Brain reads what Connect the dots has produced so far, whether or not it has finished.
- Filtering and searching narrow the same graph rather than opening a separate view.
- The graph can be read before every source has finished processing — Morgan doesn't wait for a complete vault to start looking.

## Still open

- What a filter or search that matches nothing shows isn't specified.
- Whether the Brain distinguishes a genuinely important node from one that is merely well connected is untested.
